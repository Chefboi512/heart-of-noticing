Meet Emily

I’ve always been drawn to the small things, the way sunlight moves through leaves, the way the bees dance while pollinating flowers, shadows of plants cast to the ground at golden hour…

For a long time, though, I was much better at noticing everyone else than I was at noticing myself. I was a people-pleaser who always looked outside of myself for the answers that I wanted.

Then, breathwork and nature connection began teaching me something new! How to turn my attention inward. How to listen to my body. How to sit with what I’m feeling instead of immediately trying to change it. How to trust myself a little more.

What started for me as a personal practice became a calling. I began guiding others through breathwork and meditation and intentional moments of slowing down, to reconnect with themselves.

My hope is that each session helps you feel a little more connection and a deeper trust in yourself.
